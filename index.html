Elemental Clash — VR Prototype
Left hand: thumbstick to walk, and swing your sword for melee.
Casting: A button and B button each cast their equipped spell, both from the right hand.
Right thumbstick click opens the menu. While it's open, point the right controller and:
  • Trigger a spell icon → pick it up
  • Trigger a slot (A or X) → drop it there
  • Grip → upgrade your sword (costs coins)
Walk into the blue portal to reach the graveyard. Kill zombies with your sword or spells -- they drop coins. Walk into the green portal there to come home.
