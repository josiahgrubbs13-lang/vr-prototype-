Elemental Clash — VR Prototype
Left hand: thumbstick to walk, or push off the ground/platforms to fly (no button needed, just shove your hand against a surface).
Right hand: press a button to cast.
  • Trigger → Fireball
  • Grip → Wind Blade
  • A / X button → Lightning Bolt
  • B / Y button → Mana Blast
  • Thumbstick click → Water Blast
Hit the target dummy, or throw a spell into the blue portal and watch it exit the orange one.
