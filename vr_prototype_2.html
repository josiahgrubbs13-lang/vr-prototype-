Elemental Clash — VR Prototype
Left hand: push off the ground / platforms to fly around (no button needed, just shove your hand against a surface).
Right hand: hold the GRIP button and draw a shape in the air, then release to cast.
  • Draw a circle → Fireball
  • Draw a straight slash → Wind Blade
  • Draw a zigzag → Lightning Bolt
Hit the target dummy, or throw a spell into the blue portal and watch it exit the orange one.
